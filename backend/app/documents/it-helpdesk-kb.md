# Molslinjen IT Helpdesk Knowledge Base

## VPN

**How to connect to VPN:**
1. Open the GlobalProtect application from the system tray.
2. Enter the VPN gateway address provided by IT.
3. Log in with your Molslinjen email and password.
4. Click Connect.

**VPN not connecting:**
- Ensure you have an internet connection first.
- Restart GlobalProtect and try again.
- Check that your credentials are correct (use the same password as your Molslinjen email).
- If the issue persists, contact IT via the helpdesk portal.

---

## Password Reset

**How to reset your Windows/email password:**
- Visit the self-service portal at https://myaccount.microsoft.com
- Click "Change password" and follow the steps.
- Your new password must be at least 12 characters and include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
- If you are locked out, contact IT via the helpdesk or call the IT hotline.

---

## Email (Outlook)

**Email not working / Outlook not loading:**
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Restart Outlook.
3. If Outlook shows "Disconnected" in the status bar, try: File → Account Settings → Repair.
4. If you cannot access webmail at https://outlook.office.com, contact IT.

**How to set up email on your mobile phone:**
- iPhone: Settings → Mail → Add Account → Microsoft Exchange → enter your Molslinjen email.
- Android: Open Outlook app → Add Account → enter your Molslinjen email.
- Your IT department will provide the server settings if prompted.

## Software Installation

**How to request new software:**
- Software must be approved before installation.
- Submit a software request ticket via the IT helpdesk portal.
- Include the software name, version, and business justification.
- Standard approvals take 1-3 business days.

**Installing approved software:**
- Approved software is available in the Company Portal (Windows) or Self Service (Mac).
- Open Company Portal, find the software, and click Install.

## Shared Drives / Network Drives

**How to connect to a shared drive:**
1. Open File Explorer.
2. Click "Map network drive" (This PC → Map network drive).
3. Enter the drive path in the format `\\server\sharename` (ask IT for the exact path).
4. Check "Reconnect at sign-in" and click Finish.

**Cannot access a shared drive:**
- Ensure you are connected to the VPN if working remotely.
- Check with your manager that you have been granted access.
- Submit a helpdesk ticket requesting access if needed.

---

## Printer Issues

**Printer not printing:**
1. Check that the printer is powered on and has paper.
2. Open print queue (Start → Printers & Scanners → select printer → Open queue).
3. Cancel any stuck jobs and try again.
4. Restart the Print Spooler: Services → Print Spooler → Restart.

**How to add a network printer:**
1. Go to Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → Add device.
2. If the printer does not appear, click "Add manually" and enter the printer's IP address.
3. Contact IT if you need the printer's IP address.

---

## Computer Won't Start

**PC does not boot:**
1. Check that the power cable is connected.
2. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to force shutdown, then press again to start.
3. If you see a black screen with error text, note the error message and contact IT.
4. If the PC shows the Molslinjen login screen but you cannot log in, try your email password.

## Escalation Policy

If your issue is not resolved within:
- **4 hours** for Priority 1 (critical systems down)
- **1 business day** for Priority 2 (major disruption)
- **3 business days** for Priority 3 (general requests)

Contact IT management directly or escalate via the helpdesk portal.
